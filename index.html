      A Field Guide to the Packers Fan

      Three common species, all found within 50 feet of a cheese curd. None of them have been right about anything since 2010.

      Specimen A

      The Cheesehead

      Fromagius foamheadus
      Habitat
      Lambeau parking lot, 7 a.m., already three beers deep.
      Call
      "GO PACK GO," repeated until the fourth quarter, then silence.
      Diet
      Brats, curds, Miller Lite and excuses.
      Fun fact
      Voluntarily wears a triangle of foam cheese in public. Has a mortgage.
      Specimen B

      The Historian

      Titletownus nostalgicus
      Habitat
      Any conversation about the current season, which he immediately steers to 1967.
      Call
      "We have thirteen titles." Can't name a player on any of those teams.
      Diet
      VHS tapes of Lombardi, Favre highlight reels, the past.
      Fun fact
      Describes Lambeau as "hallowed ground." It's a parking lot with a stadium in it.
      Specimen C

      The Shareholder

      Ownerus delusionalis
      Habitat
      Framed stock certificate in the basement, next to the dehumidifier.
      Call
      "Well, technically I'm an owner." It's worth nothing. It pays nothing. He paid $300 for it.
      Diet
      Annual meeting snacks and a deep sense of importance.
      Fun fact
      Owns a piece of the team and still can't get anyone to fire the kicker.
